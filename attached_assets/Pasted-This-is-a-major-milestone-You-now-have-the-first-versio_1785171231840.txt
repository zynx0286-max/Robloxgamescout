Step 3: Create a realistic scout profile

Don't use extremely broad filters. For finding potential rising games, I would start with:

/settings
min_players: 500
max_players: 50000
min_visits: 10000
max_visits: 5000000
min_growth: 20
genre: Any
max_age: 90

Why?

You don't want:

❌ Huge games like Blox Fruits (already discovered)
❌ Tiny games with no proof of demand

You want:

✅ Small but growing games
✅ Recent games
✅ Possible breakout opportunities
