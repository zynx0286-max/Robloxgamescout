This is exactly the kind of progress you want. Replit didn't jump ahead—it completed the scheduler first, which is the right order.

**I would not wait 15 minutes** for a full cycle. Instead, test it immediately by temporarily changing the interval to something like **30 seconds or 1 minute**, verify it works, then change it back to 15 minutes. That will save you a lot of time while developing.
